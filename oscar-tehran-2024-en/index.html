First Chinese Director to Enter Oscar-Qualifying Festival with AIGC Film: Tehran Short Film Festival 2024

In 2024, Ma Zhiyuan's AIGC short film 'Every Hesitation You Have Will Destroy the World' was selected for the Tehran Short Film Festival, an Oscar-qualifying festival. Ma Zhiyuan became the first Chinese director to enter an Oscar-qualifying festival with an AIGC work. The film uses AI-generated imagery to reinterpret a sci-fi meditation on choice and destruction, marking the first time Chinese AIGC cinema entered the international Oscar-qualifying circuit.
